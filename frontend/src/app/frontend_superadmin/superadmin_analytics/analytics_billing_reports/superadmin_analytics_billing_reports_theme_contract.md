# Theme Contract — Superadmin Analytics Billing Reports

## Consumed Design Tokens
- Backgrounds: `bg-background`, `bg-card`, `bg-muted`
- Text: `text-foreground`, `text-muted-foreground`
- Borders: `border-border`
- Status Accents:
  - Amber (Billing Primary): `text-amber-400`, `bg-amber-500/10`, `border-amber-500/20`
  - Emerald (Success/Paid/Revenue): `text-emerald-400`, `bg-emerald-500/10`
  - Rose (Failed/Overdue): `text-rose-400`, `bg-rose-500/10`
  - Sky (Information/Cycle): `text-sky-400`, `bg-sky-500/10`

No arbitrary hex codes or raw RGB values.
